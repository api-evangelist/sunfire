## What Sunfire is

- Legal entity: Sunfire SE (converted from GmbH to a European stock corporation, 2025).
  HRB 46479, Amtsgericht Dresden. Head office: Gasanstaltstrasse 2, 01237 Dresden, Germany.
- Additional entities/sites: Sunfire Business Services SE & Co. KG (Dresden), Sunfire Solingen GmbH
  (Solingen), and a Berlin office at the EUREF-Campus.
- Founded 2010 by Carl Berninghausen, Christian von Olshausen and Nils Aldag.
- Management Board: Nils Aldag (Chairman), Christian von Olshausen, Frank Posnanski, Jens Henneberg.
  Supervisory Board chair: Dr. Frank Mastiaux.
- Sector: renewable energy / industrial equipment manufacturing — electrolysis and power-to-X.

## Products (physical plant, not software)

- Sunfire-HyLink Alkaline — pressurized alkaline electrolysis (AEL). HyLink Alkaline 22 (10 MW
  module) and HyLink Alkaline 23 (50 MW module), operating at 30 bar(g), 5 MW AEL stack.
- Sunfire-HyLink SOEC — high-temperature solid oxide electrolysis, 10 MW modules, stated
  37.5 kWh/kg H2 (89%) efficiency for the third-generation SOEC electrolyzer.
- Scope of supply: FEED engineering, production and pre-assembly, delivery, project management,
  on-site support, maintenance and digital/remote monitoring services.

## Referenced projects

RWE (Lingen), Salzgitter AG, Neste (Rotterdam), TotalEnergies, BASF (Schwarzheide), P2X Solutions
(Finland), Nordic Ren-Gas (Tampere), MPREIS, and the Bad Lauchstaedt Energy Park.
